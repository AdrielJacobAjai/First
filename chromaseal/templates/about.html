About this tool

Presumptive only. ChromaSeal removes human-reading and lighting error from colorimetric field tests. It does not fix chemical cross-reactivity, and it is not a laboratory replacement.

  • Reference values are estimates. Neutral and primary card patches use nominal values; the three reagent patches are printed approximations from published colour descriptions, refined by averaging daylight photos. Not spectrophotometer-grade.
  • Metamerism. Printed ink can match the real reagent colour under one light and drift under another.
  • Linear correction. Accuracy degrades in extreme lighting; photos with a poor calibration fit are rejected, not guessed at.
  • Tamper evidence. Records are hash-chained (SHA-256). This makes edits detectable, not impossible.
  • This prototype is used only with printed mock swatches. Never use real drugs or reagents.
